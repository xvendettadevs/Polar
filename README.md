## 🧐 What is Polar?

**Polar** is a Chrome extension built for traders who demand an edge.

As soon as a Polymarket market appears on your screen, Polar automatically detects it and runs a full intelligence scan. No manual research, no tab switching, no wasted time. You get a comprehensive market analysis in **3-5 seconds**.

---

## ✨ What Polar Delivers

### ⚡ Instant Market Analysis
Automatic scanning of any active market. Average report generation time is between 3 and 5 seconds.

### 🧠 AI-Driven Predictions (Up to 98.2% Accuracy)
Our predictive models are powered by a fusion of:
*   Historical betting data;
*   Price action analysis;
*   Global news cycles;
*   Market sentiment.

### 🐦 Live X (Twitter) Signal Tracking
Don't miss the narrative. Polar highlights:
*   Relevant accounts and key influencers;
*   Real-time sentiment shifts;
*   Breaking news directly related to the specific market.

### 📰 Smart News Aggregation
Recent and relevant news tied directly to the market outcome. We filter the noise so you can focus on the facts.

### 📊 Historical Data & Volatility
Access historical price and probability data to analyze trends, volatility, and market stability.
